Confirm the funds on your own

You don't have to take our word for it. In Florida, surplus funds from tax deed sales and foreclosure auctions are generally held by the county Clerk of Court. You can check directly:

  1. Find your county Clerk of Court's official website. Search for the county name plus "Clerk of Court," and make sure you're on the official Clerk site rather than a link from a letter or email.
  2. Look for "surplus," "excess proceeds" or "unclaimed funds" for tax deed sales or foreclosures. Many Clerks post lists online.
  3. Call the Clerk's office using the phone number listed on its official website, and ask whether funds are being held for your property or case.
  4. Have your details ready: the property address, the parcel or case number (if your letter includes one), and the owner's name.
