Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how American Financial Recovery ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information you share with us through AmericanFinancialRecovery.com (the "Site").

Information we collect

When you complete our free case review form or letter lookup form, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address (email is optional)
  • The former property address and county
  • Your relationship to the property (for example, owner, heir, or co-owner)
  • The type of sale, approximate year of sale, and case number, if you provide them
  • The reference code from a letter we sent you, if you provide it
  • Your preferred time to be contacted, any message you choose to include, and your text message and email preferences

We may also receive basic technical information that browsers send automatically, such as the page you submitted the form from, your language setting, and the date and time of your submission.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Contact you about your inquiry and about possible surplus funds claims related to your former property
  • Research public records to determine whether surplus funds may be available
  • Prepare and follow up on a claim if you choose to work with us
  • Respond to your questions and keep you informed

Text messages and email

We will send you text messages or emails only if you check the matching box on our form, or otherwise give us permission. Consent to receive texts or emails is optional and is not a condition of using our services.

  • Text messages: Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help.
  • Email: You can opt out at any time by replying to any email with "unsubscribe" or by contacting us at US@AmericanFinancialRecovery.com.

You may also ask us at any time to stop calling you.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your mobile phone number or text message consent with third parties for their marketing purposes.

We may share information only as needed to handle your inquiry or claim. This includes service providers that help us operate the Site and receive form submissions, and county Clerks or courts when filing a claim you have authorized. We may also share information when required by law.

How we protect your information

We use reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How long we keep information

We keep your information as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, pursue a claim you have authorized, and meet our legal and business record-keeping obligations.

Your choices

You may ask to review, correct, or delete the personal information you have given us, or to stop being contacted, by reaching us using the details below.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
